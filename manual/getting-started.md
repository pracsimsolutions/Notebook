## Open the Notebook

1. Open any model in FlexSim.
2. Open the **Toolbox**.
3. Double-click **Notebook**.

The panel appears docked next to your 3D view. Drag its tab to dock it wherever you like. When you
reload the model, the Notebook reopens automatically.

## Find your way around

The tab strip runs across the top of the panel: **Scoping**, **Notes**, **Board**, **Canvases**
and **Validation**. Click a tab to switch. If the panel is too narrow to show everything, the
extra controls collapse behind a **...** button at the right end of the strip.

A small status message beside the strip tells you when your changes are being saved.

> [!TIP]
> You do not need to save anything yourself. Changes are written into the model as you work. To
> make them permanent in the file, save the model in FlexSim as usual.

## Where your notes live

Everything is stored inside the `.fsm` model file. Copy the model and the notes come with it. Nothing
is written to your user profile and nothing is sent over the network.

To move your notes into a different model, or to keep a backup outside the model, use
[Export and import](how-to/export-and-import.md).
