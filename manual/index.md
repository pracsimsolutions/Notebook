PracSim Notebook keeps your project notes next to the model they describe. It is a panel docked
beside the 3D view with five tabs, and everything you write is saved inside the `.fsm` model file,
so the notes travel with the model. There is no installer step for your data, no telemetry and no
network access.

## The tabs

| Tab | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Scoping](tabs/scoping.md) | A structured scope document: business questions, objectives, deliverables, stakeholders, assumptions and risks. |
| [Notes](tabs/notes.md) | Free-form rich-text notes, organized in a list. |
| [Board](tabs/board.md) | A Kanban board of cards for tracking work. |
| [Canvases](tabs/canvases.md) | Diagrams, layouts and annotations on an unlimited drawing surface. |
| [Validation](tabs/validation.md) | A record of each model run: inputs, outputs, changes and observations. |
